Michael Simbirsky

Secretary

Michael grew up in Kharkiv and graduated from Karazin National University. In 1991 Ukraine gained its independence, and Michael took an active part in this democratic movement.

After earning his Ph.D. in mathematics at the Institute for Low Temperature Physics, he moved to Israel and switched to software engineering. His career eventually brought him to California, where he worked at several startups and then at Google and Synopsys.

The Revolution of Dignity and the following russian aggression in 2014 led Michael to be actively involved in support of Ukraine. He helped raise funds for and coordinate humanitarian and educational projects, holding volunteer officer roles at a large American-Ukrainian nonprofit before co-founding AHF.
